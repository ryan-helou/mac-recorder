# Mac Recorder

**A one-click screen recorder for the Mac that captures video, system sound and microphone into a single file.**

Most screen recorders on the Mac make you choose between the screen, the computer's sound and your microphone, or juggle extra tools to get all three. I built Mac Recorder so one button captures everything together.

Press record and it captures what's on screen, the sound coming out of the Mac, and your microphone. Press stop and it saves a single video file to your Movies folder and shows it in Finder.

You can record the whole screen with all of the Mac's sound, or just one app's windows and its sound. The recording keeps going if you close the app's window, and everything stays on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

If you record a conversation, make sure everyone in it knows and agrees.

## Get it

Download this repository, run `./build.sh --install` in the folder, then open Mac Recorder from Applications. The first time, macOS asks for screen recording and microphone access.
